Welcome to Game Finder

1. Save your teams

Tap a team name under any game. Then tap ★ My teams at the top to see only their games.

2. Put it on your home screen

iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

Android: in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to home screen).

3. Live scores

While an NFL or college game is on, the score and clock update on their own.
